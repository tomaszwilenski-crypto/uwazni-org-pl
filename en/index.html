How does it work?

Three steps. Sign up now and we will let you know when the first tasks are ready.

1

Sign up

All you need is an email address. Before your first task you will confirm your phone number and fill in a short profile: a few questions about you, about 3 minutes.

2

Get tasks

We pick articles, websites and videos that match your profile. We will notify you when something is waiting.

3

Get paid

Every accepted task adds to your balance. Withdraw whenever you like, from PLN 20.

What does a single task look like?

  1. You get an invitationBy email or text message, with a description, duration and pay rate.
  2. You open the linkIn your usual browser, on a phone or computer. Nothing to install.
  3. You read and clickUse the website as you normally would and click on whatever interests you. Attention matters, not speed.
  4. Sometimes a short surveyA few questions after the task, paid extra.
  5. Your balance growsOnce the task is checked, the amount goes to your account.

We pay for real attention

Our results have to be reliable. That is why the rules are simple and the same for everyone.

One account per person

Duplicate accounts are blocked together with their balance.

Phone verification

One text message at sign-up. It protects your balance and the whole panel.

Check questions

Sometimes we ask about the content of the task. Attentive panelists get more tasks.

Right to appeal

Task rejected? Write to us within 14 days. Every appeal is reviewed by a person.

Your data is safe

What we collect

The profile data you give us, and whether and for how long a task was carried out.

What we don't do

We do not record your screen or camera. We do not track you outside tasks. We do not sell your personal data, and companies only receive aggregated results.

You are in control

We comply with the GDPR. We will delete your account and data on request: just write to pomoc@uwazni.org.pl. Details in the privacy policy.

Who can join?

Any adult living in Poland with a phone or computer. Most tasks are on Polish-language websites, so you need to read Polish comfortably.We are looking for people of all ages, from big cities and small towns. Doctors and pharmacists can join the specialist group after their licence to practise is verified.

Refer and earn

After your first tasks you will get your own referral link. For every person who joins through it and completes their first 3 tasks, you get PLN 10.

Frequently asked questions

Is this legal and safe?+

Yes. The panel is run by TGV Sp. z o.o., a company registered in Poland (KRS 0000534661). The rules are set out in the panel terms.

Do I need to install anything?+

No. You open tasks in your usual browser, on a phone or computer.

How much will I really earn?+

It depends on how many tasks match your profile. Every task shows its pay rate before you start.

What if a task is rejected?+

We reject a task when there was no real attention, for example the browser tab was inactive or the answer to a check question was wrong. You will be told the reason. You have 14 days to appeal, and every appeal is reviewed by a person.

Who am I browsing websites for?+

For companies and publishers who want to know how people really read and watch their content. We do not share your personal data with them.

Do I have to pay tax?+

Income from the panel may be taxable. Where the law requires it, we will send you a tax statement for the year. Details are in the panel terms.

How do I delete my account?+

Write to pomoc@uwazni.org.pl. We will delete your account and data, and pay out any balance from PLN 20 before deletion.

Where can I get help?+

Write to pomoc@uwazni.org.pl. We reply within one business day.
