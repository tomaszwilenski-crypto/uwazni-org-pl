    What analytics and surveys can't show you

    Analytics shows what people who already came did. Surveys tell you what people say. With us, the chosen group really uses your page: reads, scrolls and clicks. You see it before you spend your campaign budget.

    The study group looks like the group in Poland

    A result only makes sense if the sample matches the structure of the target group. That is why we control it at every stage.

    We build group profiles from many sources
    • What Poles say themselves
    • Statistics Poland (GUS) demographics
    • Structure of the Polish online audience
    • Attitude surveys
    • Behaviour on Polish websites
    • Attention measurement

    Quotas from GUS data

    We distribute age, gender, education and town size in line with GUS data for the whole group.

    Psychographics from real voices

    We describe the group's attitudes, needs and language based on what Poles in the group say about themselves, and validate it with quantitative research.

    Quality control

    Sessions without real attention or not meeting the criteria are excluded from the result.

    Plan and fieldwork in the report

    The report shows the planned and achieved sample structure side by side.

    What's in the report?

    Not just "how many people came". You see how your audience uses the page: where they lose interest, what they click, how many read attentively and which version wins.

    Participants click on whatever interests them, just like regular users. You see what share of the group clicked each element and how long after arriving.

      Frequently asked questions

      I already use Hotjar or Microsoft Clarity. Why do I need a study?+

      Click and scroll maps show the traffic that already reaches your page, without knowing who those people are. A study shows how the group you choose reads it, including before publication and in an A/B test on the same group. More in Attention research vs Hotjar and Clarity.

      Do I need to install anything on my website?+

      No. All you need is a publicly available page address or test access provided in the configuration.

      When will I see results?+

      Right away. The first visits appear in the Google Analytics real-time view about a minute after the study starts. Standard GA reports (source, engagement, devices) usually fill in within a few hours, and full data is available after 24–48 hours. That is how GA4 processes data. We keep you updated on study progress.

      How does the A/B test work?+

      You provide the addresses of two page versions. Each version is visited by a separate sample from the same target group, of the same size and structure. The report shows both versions side by side, section by section.

      Do participants click on the page?+

      Yes. They use the page naturally: they read, scroll and click on whatever interests them. The report shows what share of the group clicked each element. Participants do not click on ads or submit data in forms unless the task requires it.

      Will the study distort my statistics?+

      Every study visit carries the parameter utm_source=uwazni, so you can exclude it in Google Analytics with a single filter. Study traffic may not be used in audience statistics or for ad billing.

      How are the groups selected?+

      We build groups using demographic and psychographic criteria. The full description is in the Methodology section.

      Can I test a page before it goes live?+

      Yes. Just provide the address of the staging version and access details in the configuration.

      Are the groups comparable with TV planning?+

      Yes. The Adults 18–49 and 18–59 groups correspond to the commercial target groups used in Polish TV campaign planning.

      Can international companies order a study?+

      Yes. We work with companies from Poland and abroad. Participants are people living in Poland, so the study shows how Polish audiences engage with your content. We invoice in PLN, and EU businesses with a valid VAT ID are invoiced under the reverse charge mechanism.

      Who processes the data and where?+

      The service is provided by TGV Sp. z o.o., based in Warsaw, Poland. You receive study data in aggregated form, without participants' personal data. If we process data on your behalf, we sign a data processing agreement. Details in the privacy policy.
